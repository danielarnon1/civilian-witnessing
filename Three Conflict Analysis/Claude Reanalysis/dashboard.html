Heterogeneous effects by demographic group
How the tests work
  • For each outcome × characteristic: outcome ~ treatment × characteristic (OLS, HC2). A joint robust Wald test asks whether the treatment effect differs across the characteristic's groups at all.
  • Multiple testing: Benjamini–Hochberg q-values within this survey, separately for the pooled-side and by-post families. Significant = q < . Cells show q; dark cells pass, light cells have unadjusted p < .05 but do not survive correction.
  • A group enters a test only if every treatment group has at least 20 respondents in it; "–" means fewer than two groups qualified, or the question was not asked in this survey (Pr6a Israel-Palestine sympathy is asked only in the Nagorno-Karabakh and Sudan surveys). Groups under 60 respondents overall are merged or dropped.
  • Pre-treatment items (Pr1–Pr7) were asked before the post. Pr1 is open text, coded by keyword (foreign, war, Israel, Ukraine, China, terror, military, world, …) — an approximate measure. Pr2 "attention to foreign conflicts" averages the follow-the-news items for Israel-Hamas, Ukraine, Sudan, Myanmar and (where asked) Nagorno-Karabakh, split at the survey median.
